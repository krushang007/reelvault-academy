    5 High-Contrast Font Pairings for Modern Design Projects

    Carousel by @designs_by_max 7 slidesEnglishshared 8 Oct 2026

    Library / Design / Typography

    A curated swipe file of five versatile typeface combinations balancing distinct heading personalities with workhorse body text across tech, editorial, e-commerce, and boutique branding. Each pair establishes immediate typographic hierarchy without sacrificing body legibility.

    Steps

    1. Geometric Tech / Modern Display: Pair Unbounded (display heading) with Albert Sans (body). The bold, wide geometric presence of Unbounded anchors headers while Albert Sans provides high-density screen legibility. (slide 2)
    2. Clean Digital Product / SaaS (Recommended): Pair Urbanist (heading) with Open Sans (body). Urbanist delivers contemporary sharp geometric architecture, while Open Sans keeps long-form body copy open and neutral. (slide 3)
    3. Expanded Brand Cohesion: Pair Zalando Sans Expanded (heading) with Zalando Sans (body). Using width variation from the same type family guarantees tonal harmony while creating dramatic scale contrast. (slide 4)
    4. Modern Editorial & Luxury: Pair Gloock (high-contrast display serif heading) with Inter (clean UI grotesque body). Delivers high-end editorial prestige in the titles while maintaining modern application clarity in body text. (slide 5)
    5. Artisanal & Warm Hospitality: Pair Yellowtail (flowing script heading) with Nunito (rounded sans-serif body). Balances casual handcrafted script titles with soft, friendly geometric body typography. (slide 6)

    Try this

    • In Figma or CSS, set an H1 with Gloock and body copy in Inter at 16px/1.6 line-height to test editorial high contrast.
    • For clean SaaS dashboards, pair Urbanist (font-weight: 700, letter-spacing: -0.02em) with Open Sans (font-weight: 400).
