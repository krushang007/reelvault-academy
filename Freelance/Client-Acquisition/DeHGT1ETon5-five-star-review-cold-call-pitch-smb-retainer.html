    The 5-Star Review Cold Pitch & Retainer Structure for Google Maps SMBs

    Shared by @shanepduggan (Shane Duggan) • Rank #126 • Shortcode: DeHGT1ETon5

    Selling web design to local businesses usually fails because cold calls pitch abstract services to skeptical owners who hang up immediately. Shane Duggan's framework flips the cold interaction by opening with emotional validation ("Nobody hangs up on their own 5-star review"). By filtering Google Maps for established contractors (4.8+ stars, 80+ reviews, no website), auto-generating a functional mockup featuring their top customer review in the hero section, and packaging a recurring AI receptionist retainer, this model converts cold outreach into a recurring revenue business.

    Video Visual Stills

    The 5-Step Acquisition Funnel

    1

    Google Maps Qualification Filter

    Search specific high-ticket trades (Roofers, HVAC, Foundation Repair, Hardscaping). Scroll to pages 2–4 and filter for:

    • Rating: 4.8+ Stars
    • Review Count: 80+ Reviews
    • Website Link: Missing / None

    Rationale: A business with 80+ reviews is cash-flow positive and overwhelmed with work, but leaking hundreds of qualified organic search leads every month.

    2

    The 60-Second AI Pre-Build

    Generate the website before making contact. Extract their logo, business hours, service list, and photos directly from their Google Business Profile. Position their glowing customer review front-and-center in the hero banner.

    3

    The "Dave's Review" Cold Hook

    "Hey [Owner], Dave said you guys are literally the best roofers he's ever hired. I saw that review and built you a quick website that shows it off beautifully so local homeowners can actually find you. Do you have 60 seconds to see it on your phone?"

    Psychology: You are not selling a website; you are celebrating their workmanship. Calling attention to praise lowers defensive sales barriers immediately.

    4

    The Feature Demo: AI Receptionist & Booking

    Demonstrate that the site is not just static brochureware: show an integrated AI chat agent that answers emergency inquiries after-hours, qualifies project scope, and schedules estimates straight to their calendar.

    5

    Package Pricing & Compounding Retainer Model

    Deliverable Type Price
    Turnkey Website Handover One-time Setup $1,500
    Cloud Hosting + AI Inbound Receptionist Monthly Retainer $300 / mo
    Programmatic Local Service Area SEO Pages Monthly Add-on $500 / mo

    The Compounding Unit Economics

    Selling just 7 sites in 30 days (< 2 per week):

    • Immediate upfront revenue: $10,500
    • Monthly recurring baseline: $2,100 / month (or $5,600 / mo with local SEO add-on)

    The monthly retainers ensure you start every subsequent month with locked cash flow before pitching a single new client.
