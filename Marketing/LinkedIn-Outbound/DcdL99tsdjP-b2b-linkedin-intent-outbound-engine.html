    Signal-Based B2B LinkedIn Outbound Engine

    Post by @do_it_shubh 59 sEnglishshared 13 Oct 2026

    Library / Marketing / LinkedIn-Outbound

    A 4-step B2B outbound methodology replacing static list-scraping with real-time intent triggers (funding rounds, leadership appointments, hiring surges) on LinkedIn Sales Navigator. The framework researches prospects past their headline to map ICP requirements and recent wins, pre-warms targets via post engagement, sends contextual non-salesy DMs, and sustains multi-touch follow-ups with case studies. The creator pitches a done-for-you lead generation service at the conclusion.

    Steps

    1. Build Lists on Intent Triggers: In LinkedIn Sales Navigator, filter targets by dynamic change triggers rather than static titles alone. Select target headcount bands (1–10, 11–50, 51–200), department roles (e.g. Business Development / Finance), and activate the Changed jobs and Posted on LinkedIn toggles under Recent Updates. (00:06–00:11)
    2. Research Deep Context: Analyze prospects past their headline or bio. Review their recent post themes, interviews they gave or attended, pain points they voiced, and company milestones (e.g. funding rounds, new product launches). (00:15–00:27)
    3. Draft a 1-on-1 Contextual DM: Reference their exact thesis or recent post in the opening sentence. Acknowledge what they built, validate their approach, and position your system as a tool that automates their exact manual process. (00:28–00:34)
    4. Warm Up Before Connecting: Like and meaningfully interact with the prospect's most recent post before transmitting a connection request or direct message. (00:35–00:40)
    5. Multi-Touch Follow-Up Sequencing: Keep outbound conversations alive by sending engaging assets (specific case studies, revenue proof, teardown PDFs, and relevant industry memes) instead of generic follow-up reminders. (00:41–00:46)
