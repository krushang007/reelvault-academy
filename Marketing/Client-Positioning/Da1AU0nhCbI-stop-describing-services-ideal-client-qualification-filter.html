    Client Positioning: The "You'll Enjoy Working With Us If" Qualification Framework

    Shared by @gentlerainman • Rank #130 • Shortcode: Da1AU0nhCbI

    Most agency and freelance websites suffer from feature-focused commoditization: they list generic service capabilities ("We build custom web apps", "We design responsive digital experiences") that force prospects to evaluate them solely on price. By shifting from describing your services to describing your ideal client through a dedicated "You'll probably enjoy working with us if..." section, you repel low-budget micromanagers while magnetically attracting high-fit partners.

    Video Visual Stills

    The Psychological Shift: Service vs. Client Qualification

    ❌ The Generic Service Trap

    "We provide comprehensive digital strategy, full-stack software development, and UI/UX design."

    Result: You look identical to 10,000 competing agencies. Prospects bargain and treat you like a vendor.

    ✅ The Client Mirror Filter

    "You'll probably enjoy working with us if you value speed over committee consensus, have product-market fit, and want an autonomous partner who speaks in business outcomes."

    Result: Ideal buyers self-identify immediately; wrong-fit prospects self-select out before booking calls.

    Copywriting Template for Agency & Freelance Portfolios

    The 4-Part Filter Architecture

    1. Operating Cadence: Specify how you work best (e.g., async-first via Slack/Loom, weekly sprints, zero redundant status meetings).
    2. Stage & Budget Reality: Define where the client is (e.g., Series A funded, $1M+ ARR, or established local operators ready to invest $5k+).
    3. Decision-Making Style: Require a single empowered stakeholder with authority to approve milestones.
    4. The Explicit Disqualifier: "You will NOT enjoy working with us if you need daily phone calls, require RFP bidding rounds, or need someone to execute line-by-line without strategic input."
