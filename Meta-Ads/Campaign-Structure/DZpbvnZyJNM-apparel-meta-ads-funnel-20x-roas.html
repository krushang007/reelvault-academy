    Full-Funnel Meta Ads Structure for 20X+ ROAS in E-Commerce Apparel

    Reel by @themamtacreator 146 sHinglishshared 26 Oct 2026

    Library / Meta-Ads / Campaign-Structure

    A live breakdown of a 22.7X ROAS Meta Ads structure for an Indian women's apparel brand (Jyoti Sarees). On a ₹47,779 spend, the active review campaign generated ₹10,85,090 in sales. The architecture uses a staged funnel: TOF cold prospecting with Bollywood celebrity interests and recent visitor exclusions, MOF retargeting built on social engagers and customer WhatsApp feedback videos, BOF catalog dynamic ads featuring bestsellers only, and interest A/B testing (Diet vs Foodie).

    Steps

    1. Top of Funnel (TOF) Prospecting: Launch cold interest campaigns targeting women aged 18–65+ with high-affinity celebrity/actor interests. Crucially, exclude recent visitors (PM | Wv 30 Days) so cold budget is never cannibalized by retargeting. (00:37–00:49)
    2. Placement Restriction: Restrict ad placements to Instagram feeds and reels if historical engagement and conversion data shows Instagram drives nearly all organic and paid sales. (00:50–00:56)
    3. Creative Selection from Organic Winners: Do not guess ad creatives; pull organically proven Instagram reels (e.g. saree styling reels) directly into the sales campaigns. (00:57–01:04)
    4. Middle of Funnel (MOF) Retargeting: Build a custom audience combining 365-day Instagram account engagers (PM | Insta 365), 365-day Facebook engagers (PM | FB 365), and 30-day website visitors (PM | Wv 30 Days). (01:05–01:21)
    5. Social Proof Creative: Create video collages from real customer WhatsApp chat screenshots, unboxing pictures, and 5-star ratings ("Happy Customer Feedback"). In this account, this single creative delivered 21x ROAS. (01:22–01:36)
    6. Bottom of Funnel (BOF) Catalog Ads: After accumulating 15–20 days of pixel data, launch Advantage+ Catalog ads filtered exclusively to proven bestsellers. This catalog campaign generated ₹32,19,278 on ₹91,153 spend (35.32x ROAS). (01:37–01:46)
    7. Interest Angle A/B Testing: Test audience hypotheses head-to-head (e.g. Diet interests vs Foodie interests) while excluding existing 30-day visitors and purchasers. When one segment clearly wins (Diet won decisively here), cut the losing segment immediately. (01:48–02:16)

    Try this

    • In your cold TOF campaigns, add a negative exclusion for Website visitors (30 days) to prevent retargeting overlap.
    • Collect genuine customer WhatsApp messages, reviews, and unboxing images, and build a dedicated 1:1 or 9:16 video creative specifically for your MOF audience.
    • When running Catalog Sales ads, filter the product set to proven bestsellers rather than advertising your entire catalog.
