    Meta Creator Marketing Hub: Discovering Influencers via First-Party Audience Overlap

    Harshit Singh (@marketing.harshit) • 2026-10-05 • Original Reel

    A performance-marketing workflow for influencer discovery inside Meta Ads Manager. Instead of selecting creators based on vanity follower counts, advertisers use the native Creator Marketing Hub to cross-reference custom audience signals (e.g. 180-day website visitors, past purchasers, high-intent CRM lists) against creator follower graphs, unlocking data-backed creators for high-converting Partnership Ads.

    Steps / Workflow

    1. Navigate to Creator Marketing Hub: Open Meta Ads Manager → click the hamburger icon (All tools) in the left navigation sidebar → under the Advertise section, click Creator Marketing Hub.
    2. Access the Creator Discovery Interface: In Creator Marketing Hub, select Discover creators from the left menu to view creator cards and filtering parameters.
    3. Apply Baseline Demographic Filters (Optional): Narrow down creators by Country (e.g. India, United States), Follower size bands (Under 10K, 10K–25K, 25K–50K, 50K–90K, Over 90K), Interests, Age brackets, and Gender.
    4. Open the Audience Filter Modal: On the top-right filter toolbar, click the Audience button to open the “Target audience filter” dialog.
    5. Inject First-Party Custom Audiences: Under Ads Manager audiences, select your highest-intent custom audience (e.g., Website Visitors 180 Days or August Customer List).
    6. Query Overlapping Creators: Click Show creators. Meta cross-references your selected custom audience with creator follower graphs, surfacing creators whose audiences possess high affinity and direct overlap with your buyers.
    7. Execute via Partnership Ads: Reach out directly through the portal (“Contact” button) to commission native UGC and run official Meta Partnership Ads (formerly Branded Content Ads) where the ad displays both the creator and brand handles.

    Meta has a Creator Marketplace feature that can help match your brand with creators based on your existing audience signals.

    You already have a Custom Audience of people who have purchased from you, engaged with your brand, or shown strong intent.
    Instead of randomly searching for a creator and hoping their audience matches your ICP...
    You can use your audience as a signal to discover creators whose audience may overlap with the people you actually want to reach.

    That changes the entire influencer marketing game.
    Because now the creator isn’t just giving you:
    ❌ A face
    ❌ A video
    ❌ Followers

    They can potentially bring:
    ✅ Relevant audience overlap
    ✅ Creator credibility
    ✅ Native content
    ✅ Partnership Ads
    ✅ A stronger audience signal for paid distribution

    The goal isn’t to find the creator with the biggest following.
    It’s to find the creator whose audience looks most like the people you want to sell to.
    And then you can test that creator’s content through Partnership Ads instead of relying only on organic reach.
    This is how you start treating influencer marketing like performance marketing. 🚀

    Tools & Features

    • Meta Creator Marketing Hub: Integrated discovery and relationship management tool within Meta Business Suite / Ads Manager.
    • Target Audience Filter: Proprietary algorithm that scores creators based on follower overlap with Meta Custom Audiences (Pixel events, Customer lists, Engagement audiences).
    • Meta Partnership Ads: Ad format enabling an advertiser to amplify creator-published posts directly with dual attribution headers and performance tracking.

    Try This

    1. Export a high-LTV customer list from Shopify/CRM containing customers who bought 2+ times.
    2. Upload as a Customer List Custom Audience inside Meta Events Manager / Audiences.
    3. Open All tools → Creator Marketing Hub → Discover creators.
    4. Click Audience, select that repeat customer audience, and filter for creators in your country with 10k–50k followers.
    5. Shortlist 5 creators with high overlap scores and request creator partnership ad access.

    Open Questions

    • Availability of Creator Marketing Hub varies by country and business verification status; accounts without active Instagram business asset linkage may not see the tab under Advertise.
    • Minimum audience size threshold: Meta typically requires custom audiences to contain sufficient matched users (>1,000 active profiles) before audience overlap calculation yields statistically meaningful creator recommendations.
