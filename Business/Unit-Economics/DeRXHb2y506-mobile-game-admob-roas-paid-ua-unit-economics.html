    Mobile Game Unit Economics: Google AdMob Revenue vs Paid User Acquisition ROAS

    Executive Summary

    Indie game developers frequently believe that launching a hyper-casual or puzzle game on Google Play and driving automated Google Ads install campaigns will generate passive profit. However, without strict target ROAS (Return on Ad Spend) bidding and lifetime value (LTV) optimization, paid install campaigns burn capital at an unsustainable loss.

    In this real financial audit of his indie puzzle game Arrow Puzzle Max, developer Pradeep reveals the exact daily spend, install velocity, and AdMob banner/interstitial revenue across an 8-day test period, diagnosing a 78% loss margin and outlining the mathematical pivot required to achieve breakeven.

    Core Takeaways & The Turnaround Strategy

    1. The "Max Installs" Bidding Trap

    When launching mobile ad campaigns, Google's default recommendation is "Maximize Conversions / Installs". The bidding algorithm optimizes solely to find devices willing to tap install, regardless of device quality, ad engagement propensity, or geo-tier value. This results in users who download, open once for 30 seconds, and never trigger high-eCPM rewarded ads.

    2. Switching to Target ROAS (tROAS)

    Instead of bidding for raw installs, hyper-casual developers must link Firebase Analytics and Google AdMob to pass real-time ad revenue events back to Google Ads. This enables tROAS bidding:

    • Google Ads targets users who historically watch rewarded ads or engage with high-value interstitial banners.
    • Campaign bids dynamically scale down when ad impression eCPMs decline.

    3. The Minimum Viable Spend Discipline

    Scaling spend from ₹2,000/day to ₹10,000/day while running at a 22% ROAS simply accelerates bankruptcy. The correct scaling rule:

    1. Reduce daily spend to the minimum viable test budget (e.g. ₹500/day).
    2. Optimize in-game ad placements (add rewarded revives, remove disruptive mid-level banners that degrade Day-1 retention).
    3. Only when organic + paid Day-7 ROAS approaches 100% (breakeven) should daily budgets be expanded to ₹5,000–₹10,000.
