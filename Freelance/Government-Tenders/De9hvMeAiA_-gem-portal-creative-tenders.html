    Finding video/design tenders on India's GeM portal

    Reel by @creativebykrishna 46 sHinglishshared 26 Oct 2026

    Library / Freelance / Government-Tenders

    Government buyers (President's Secretariat, Assam, Civil Aviation, Defence) publish tenders for videographers, editors, designers and content teams on GeM. The rules favour small businesses — a 25% MSE purchase quota and a 10-day payment default — but big tenders have high eligibility bars, some bids override the payment terms, and most go to the lowest price.

    Steps

    1. Open GeM BidPlus and search creative-service bids ("Videographer cum editor", "Content Creation Team", "Graphic Designer").
    2. Check eligibility before anything else — the Rashtrapati Bhavan bid needs ₹50 lakh turnover and 3 years' experience. (caption)
    3. Read the payment clause of each bid. Default is 10 days with 1%/month interest if late, but bids can override it — the Assam bid says 45 days. (00:36–00:39)
    4. Register as an MSE seller to count toward the 25% small-business quota. Registration is covered in the creator's Part 2, not here.

    Tools & links

    • GeM / BidPlus — government e-marketplace where departments publish tenders — gem.gov.in

    Try this

    • Search BidPlus for open creative/multimedia bids and note eligibility and payment terms for 3–5 of them.
    • Check whether MSE/Udyam registration is worth doing before bidding.
