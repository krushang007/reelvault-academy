    4 Go-To Web Design Inspiration Directories: Godly, Land-book, Httpster & Siteinspire

    Author: @rnpminds Source: Instagram Reel DeZ29VnuxWp Extracted: 2026-10-27

    A curated directory breakdown of four specialized web galleries used by modern product and branding studios to calibrate visual standards and discover non-generic layouts. Segmented by design objective: Godly for experimental micro-interactions and WebGL; Land-book for full-funnel single-page brand narratives; Httpster for typographic contrast and minimal Swiss layouts; and Siteinspire for high-end editorial portfolios.

    Directory Breakdown & Specific Use Cases

    1. 01. Godly (godly.website): Purpose: Cutting-edge and experimental web design. Features live web recordings, high-performance interactions, kinetic typography, WebGL shaders, and dark-mode SaaS interfaces. Ideal for studying fluid motion design, cursor effects, and high-production-value landing pages.
    2. 02. Land-book (land-book.com): Purpose: Single-page brand storytelling and commercial layouts. Archives complete full-page vertical screenshots of SaaS landing pages, agency portfolios, and consumer product websites. Best used for studying narrative flow, pricing section architectures, and CTA placement.
    3. 03. Httpster (httpster.net): Purpose: Clean layouts and typography combinations. An editorial archive filtering websites by typographic craftsmanship, asymmetric editorial grids, and minimal brutalist styling without visual clutter.
    4. 04. Siteinspire (siteinspire.com): Purpose: Premium and editorial web design. A longstanding design showcase categorizing websites by granular navigation patterns, layout structures, and industry verticals. Best for architecture studios, luxury brands, and art publications.
