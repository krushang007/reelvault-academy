    Using Meta's 'Describe Your Audience' AI Feature with ChatGPT

    Reel by @marketingpulse.pro 49 sHinglishshared 26 Oct 2026

    Library / Meta-Ads / AI-Targeting

    A practical breakdown of Meta's native "Describe your audience" AI feature inside Ads Manager. Instead of manually guessing detailed targeting keywords or suffering broad algorithmic drift, advertisers use a structured ChatGPT prompt template to generate a cohesive ~1,500-character persona specification (demographics, inclusion criteria, explicit exclusions, and behavioral signals), which Meta's engine parses into exact verified interest categories.

    Steps

    1. Diagnose Targeting Mismatch: Advertisers frequently waste ad budget when basic age/gender criteria fail to constrain Meta's delivery algorithms away from low-intent viewers. (00:00–00:12)
    2. Open the AI Audience Field: In Meta Ads Manager, edit your campaign ad set. Under the Detailed targeting section, click the Describe your audience tab to open the Tell us about your ideal audience field (supports up to 2,000 characters). (00:20–00:25)
    3. Generate Persona via ChatGPT: Paste the audience strategist prompt into ChatGPT, filling in your specific Industry/Niche, Product/Service, Target Region, Ideal Audience, Must-Exclude Criteria, and Campaign Objective. (00:26–00:30)
    4. Paste into Meta Ads: Copy ChatGPT's formatted natural language description (e.g. ~1,479 characters defining full-time employment, educational scope, explicit exclusions of college faculty) directly into Meta's text box. (00:31–00:36)
    5. Click 'Find options': Press the green Find options button. Meta automatically evaluates the semantic description and matches it against its active interest database, populating specific job titles and interests (e.g. Primary School Teacher, Middle School Teacher, High School Teacher, Educational technology). (00:37–00:43)

    Try this

    • When testing a new niche in Meta Ads, avoid guessing 1-2 generic interests. Run your product definition through the audience strategist prompt and test the generated description against Meta's suggestions.
    • Include explicit negative constraints in your ChatGPT prompt (e.g. "exclude part-time and college faculty") to force the generated narrative to specify clean negative boundaries.
