    6 Animated Explainer Video Patterns with Claude Opus

    Carousel by @claudetipsandtricks 8 video slidesEnglishshared 25 Oct 2026

    Library / Video-Creation / AI-Explainers

    Commercial animation studios charge $1,000+ for 30-second explainer animations. Claude Opus can generate these exact motion graphic patterns as animated code artifacts (HTML/SVG/CSS/JS) directly from concise prompts. This guide documents the 6 core explainer patterns: step-by-step process flows, panning date timelines, before/after comparison wipes, dashboard zoom highlights, system hub diagrams, and 3D myth-vs-fact flip cards.

    Steps

    1. Process Flow (Slide 2): When explaining workflows, request sequential arrow reveals so each step appears on its own beat.
      Animate these 4 steps as a flow. Draw each arrow on its own beat. (slide 2)
    2. Panning Timeline (Slide 3): For company history or multi-year roadmaps, prompt a camera pan across a horizontal milestone line.
      Animate these 5 dates on a timeline. Pan along as each one lands. (slide 3)
    3. Before / After Wipe (Slide 4): Contrast legacy states against improvements using a timed horizontal wipe transition.
      Wipe from the old homepage to the new one, left to right, 2s. (slide 4)
    4. Detail Zoom & Highlight Ring (Slide 5): Direct viewer attention across complex data visualizations by zooming 3x into the focal KPI.
      Start on the full dashboard, then zoom 3x into the revenue card. (slide 5)
    5. Hub & Spoke Architecture (Slide 6): Map integrations and connected systems by placing the core engine at the center and drawing connector lines outwards.
      Put Claude in the center. Connect Slack, Notion, Drive and GitHub. (slide 6)
    6. 3D Spring Card Flip (Slide 7): Hook viewers and dispel industry misconceptions with a 3D flip card alternating between myth and reality.
      Card flip: myth on the front, fact on the back, 1s spring. (slide 7)
