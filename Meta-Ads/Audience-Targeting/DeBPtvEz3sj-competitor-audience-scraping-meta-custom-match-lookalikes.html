    Meta-Ads · Audience-Targeting

    Competitor Audience Extraction & Meta Custom Match / 1% Lookalike Scaling

    Shared by @futurebuilders.co • Rank #132 • Shortcode: DeBPtvEz3sj

    Most digital advertisers waste thousands on cold interest-based targeting, hoping Meta's algorithm finds their ideal buyers. Top performance marketers bypass the cold learning phase by extracting active followers of established direct competitors, exporting the verified contact data into formatted customer match lists, and leveraging Meta's hash matching to deploy high-intent custom audiences and 1% Lookalikes.

    Video Visual Stills

    The 4-Step Competitor Targeting Workflow

    1

    Enrich & Extract Competitor Followers

    Using an audience scraping/enrichment tool (e.g. Mailerfind, Clay, or PhantomBuster), select Instagram and configure targeting for "Account Followers". Target competitors whose followers match your ideal buyer profile and possess commercial intent.

    2

    Clean & Format Hash Identifiers

    Format the scraped data into a clean CSV matching Meta's schema requirements:

    • email (lowercase, trimmed whitespace)
    • phone (E.164 international format with country code)
    • fn and ln (First and last name)
    3

    Create Custom Audience in Meta Ads Manager

    Navigate to Meta Ads Manager → Audiences → Create Audience → Custom Audience → Customer List. Upload your CSV file without customer value. Meta performs client-side SHA-256 hashing to cross-reference entries against active Meta user accounts.

    4

    Deploy Direct Offer & Generate 1% Lookalike

    Two-pronged scaling strategy:

    • Direct Intercept Campaign: Serve aggressive comparison and switch-over offers directly to the custom match list (typically 2,000–10,000 users).
    • 1% Lookalike Campaign: Build a 1% Lookalike from the matched seed list to target the top ~2.5 million most demographically and behaviorally identical prospects in your target country.
